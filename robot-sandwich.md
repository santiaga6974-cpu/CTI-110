# Robot Sandwich Instructions

## Assumptions

- The robot is standing at a clean table with two empty, clean hands.
- The robot can grip, lift, move, and release objects without crushing them.
- A clean, empty plate is centered on the table within reach.
- Two slices of bread are lying separately on a clean cutting board to the left of the plate. No packaging remains on either slice.
- One slice of cheese is lying flat on a clean cutting board to the right of the plate. Its wrapper has already been removed.
- All ingredients are ready to eat. This sandwich requires no spreading, cutting, or heating.
- "Top" means the surface facing the ceiling. "Centered" means the centers of the objects line up vertically.
- To grip a slice, hold its edge gently between the right thumb and fingers. Keep the slice flat while moving it.
- To release a slice, open the gripping fingers after the slice rests on the specified surface.

## Steps

1. Grip the edge of the bread slice closest to the plate with the right hand.
2. Lift that bread slice 5 centimeters above the cutting board.
3. Move that bread slice horizontally until it is centered over the plate.
4. Lower that bread slice until its bottom surface rests on the plate.
5. Release that bread slice.
6. Grip the edge of the cheese slice with the right hand.
7. Lift the cheese slice 5 centimeters above its cutting board.
8. Move the cheese slice horizontally until it is centered over the bread on the plate.
9. Lower the cheese slice until its bottom surface rests on the bread.
10. Release the cheese slice.
11. Grip the edge of the remaining bread slice with the right hand.
12. Lift that bread slice 5 centimeters above its cutting board.
13. Move that bread slice horizontally until it is centered over the cheese on the plate.
14. Lower that bread slice until its bottom surface rests on the cheese.
15. Release that bread slice.

**Done When:** The plate holds one stack containing a bottom bread slice, a cheese slice, and a top bread slice, in that order, and both robot hands are empty.
